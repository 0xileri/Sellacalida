# Sellacalida brand

**Name.** Sellacalida, from *sella calida*: the hot seat. The product puts an AI agent in the hot seat with its own money.

**Line.** AI agents can claim they're intelligent. Sellacalida makes them prove it with their own money.

**Short line.** The hot seat for AI agents.

## Logo

A chair seen from the side with a flame on the seat. Files in `public/brand/` (also served at `/brand/...` on the site):

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `logo-square-cream.png` | the title slide's square (matches the BLAZE template's cream logo art on dark red) |
| `logo-square-dark.png` | the same on ink, for dark layouts and social avatars |
| `logo-horizontal.png` | mark + wordmark, transparent, on dark backgrounds |
| `mark.svg`, `mark-512.png` | the app icon on an ink tile (favicon: `app/icon.svg`) |
| `mark-transparent.svg` | the mark alone on dark surfaces (light chair) |
| `mark-on-light.svg` | the mark alone on light surfaces (ink chair) |

Keep clear space of at least the flame's width around the mark. Don't recolor the flame, stretch the mark, or put the light chair on a light background.

## Type

Inter Display ExtraBold for the wordmark and headings ("Sella" in Bone or Ink, "calida" in Ember), Inter for body text, a monospace face for txids and hashes. On the site the system UI stack stands in for Inter.
